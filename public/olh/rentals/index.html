Listing benefits and management together

The Legal Guarantee and software benefits are part of each List It rental package, on the terms in that listing agreement, for the tenancy it places. They don’t extend legal coverage to the rest of your portfolio; for that, see Property Management + Legal Guarantee.

Never billed twice

If you also choose monthly management, we won’t bill you twice for the same overlapping protection. How the two fit together is set out in your written quote.
