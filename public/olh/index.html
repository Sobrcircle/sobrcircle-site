Good to know before you call.

What do I get each month?

Property Management + Legal Guarantee (from $199): we run the tenancy, and our licensed paralegals’ professional fees on eligible new Landlord and Tenant Board matters are included for every enrolled unit, with no activation fee. Property Management (from $149): rent administration and statements, arrears follow-up and the N4 process, tenant communication and routine maintenance coordination. OLH Landlord Shield (from $59): the LandlordEzy Automation Suite in your own account, with N4 and N1 notices, Equifax rent reporting, screening, leases and inspections, plus a rent ledger our team checks every month, while you manage your tenants. Every plan includes a 15-minute paralegal consultation each month. Prices are monthly, plus HST.

How is the monthly price worked out?

Your first tenancy has a first-unit rate and every additional tenancy costs less. We count separately rented tenancies, not street addresses, so a duplex is two tenancies. The first-unit rate applies once per owner account, not once per city. The calculator prices 1 to 10 tenancies; mixed or larger portfolios are quoted.

Does LandlordEzy serve or file notices for me?

No. With Landlord Shield you prepare N4 and N1 notices yourself in LandlordEzy, filled from your ledger, so it can take around 60 seconds when your records are accurate. Then you review, e-sign and serve them by a permitted method. There’s no OLH drafting fee for that. The software doesn’t guarantee a notice is valid or file at the LTB; professional review, service or representation is a separate paid service, or a member case for an L1 if eligible.

What does the Legal Guarantee cover?

Professional work by our licensed paralegal team on eligible new Landlord and Tenant Board matters for all enrolled units: notices, eligible applications including L1, evidence and disclosure, settlement, hearing representation and order review. Government fees, sheriff, process servers and approved outside costs are extra. It isn’t rent reimbursement, and no result or timeline is guaranteed.

I already have a tenant problem. Can I just upgrade?

A dispute that already exists isn’t absorbed by starting or upgrading a plan. We can take it on as a separate eviction engagement from $1,495 + HST and disbursements for an ordinary non-payment matter through one hearing, and you can join for the future. Landlord Shield and Property Management members whose problem began after the affected unit was enrolled may qualify for the member case rate from $995, once per owner account in any rolling 12 months.

Can I cancel?

It depends on the plan. OLH Landlord Shield is month-to-month from the start: cancel before your next renewal and it ends at the end of the month you’ve paid for. Property Management plans start with a 90-day initial period, then continue month-to-month; either side can end them with 30 calendar days’ written notice, which can be given during the initial period, so the earliest ordinary end date is day 90. When a plan ends we hand over records and open matters properly.

Can someone visit my property?

Yes. Local visits are booked when you need them, at a set per-visit price, subject to availability and confirmed with you before anyone attends. Each standard visit comes with dated photos and a report in your LandlordEzy account. Visits aren’t part of the monthly fee. Visit prices

Do you find tenants too?

Yes, through List It Realty Brokerage Inc.: Basic and Premium MLS® rental listings, or full-service placement for one month’s rent. Placement is separate from monthly management. Leasing prices

Included with OLH Landlord Shield

From missed rent to a served N4.

In your own account. LandlordEzy fills the official N4 from your rent ledger and works out the arrears, so you can prepare one in around 60 seconds when your ledger is accurate. You review it, sign it and serve it. No OLH drafting fee for notices you prepare yourself.
  1. 1Rent is missedYou get a rent-unpaid alert, and the arrears are already in your ledger.
  2. 2Review the auto-filled N4Periods, amounts and the termination date come from your ledger. Check every detail before you sign.
  3. 3E-sign and serveServe by a method the rules allow; email only with the tenant’s written consent. LandlordEzy generates the Certificate of Service.
  4. 4Filing remindersReminders around the termination and filing dates, so the next step isn’t missed.
  5. 5Optional: the L1 and the hearingIf the tenant doesn’t pay, ask for our licensed paralegals at the member case rate from $995 + HST and disbursements, if eligible. No upgrade required.
Also included: the N1 rent increase. The new rent and earliest legal effective date are pre-filled. You e-sign and serve, and the increase applies to your rent ledger on its effective date.

LandlordEzy is software: it doesn’t guarantee a notice is valid, serve paper notices for you or file at the LTB, and no eviction timeline is promised. Professional review, service or representation is a separate paid service.
