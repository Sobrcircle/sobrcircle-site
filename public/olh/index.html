Good to know before you call.

What do I get each month?

OLH Landlord Shield (from $49): the LandlordEzy Automation Suite in your own account, with N4 and N1 notices, Equifax rent reporting, screening, leases and inspections, plus a rent ledger our team checks every month and a 15-minute paralegal consultation each month, while you manage your tenants. Property Management (from $99): rent administration and statements, arrears follow-up and the N4 process, tenant communication and routine maintenance coordination. Property Management + Legal Guarantee (from $149): all of that, plus our licensed paralegal team on eligible new Landlord and Tenant Board matters for every enrolled unit, with no activation fee. Prices are monthly, plus HST.

How is the monthly price worked out?

Your first tenancy has a first-unit rate and every additional tenancy of the same type costs less. We count separately rented tenancies, not street addresses, so a duplex is two tenancies. The first-unit rate applies once per owner account, not once per city. The calculator prices 1 to 10 tenancies; mixed or larger portfolios are quoted.

Does LandlordEzy serve or file notices for me?

No. With Landlord Shield you prepare N4 and N1 notices yourself in LandlordEzy, filled from your ledger, so it can take around 60 seconds when your records are accurate. Then you review, e-sign and serve them by a permitted method. There’s no OLH drafting fee for that. The software doesn’t guarantee a notice is valid or file at the LTB; professional review, service or representation is a separate paid service, or a member case for an L1 if eligible.

What does the Legal Guarantee cover?

Professional work by our licensed paralegal team on eligible new Landlord and Tenant Board matters for all enrolled units: notices, eligible applications including L1, evidence and disclosure, settlement, hearing representation and order review. Government fees, sheriff, process servers and approved outside costs are extra. It isn’t rent reimbursement, and no result or timeline is guaranteed.

I already have a tenant problem. Can I just upgrade?

A dispute that already exists isn’t absorbed by starting or upgrading a plan. We can take it on as a separate eviction engagement from $1,495 + HST and disbursements for an ordinary non-payment matter through one hearing, and you can join for the future. Landlord Shield and Property Management members whose problem began after the affected unit was enrolled may qualify for the member case rate from $995, once per owner account in any rolling 12 months.

Can I cancel?

It depends on the plan. OLH Landlord Shield is month-to-month from the start: cancel before your next renewal and it ends at the end of the month you’ve paid for. Property Management plans start with a 90-day initial period, then continue month-to-month; either side can end them with 30 calendar days’ written notice, which can be given during the initial period, so the earliest ordinary end date is day 90. When a plan ends we hand over records and open matters properly.

Can someone visit my property?

Yes. Local visits are booked when you need them, at a set per-visit price, subject to availability and confirmed with you before anyone attends. Each standard visit comes with dated photos and a report in your LandlordEzy account. Visits aren’t part of the monthly fee. Visit prices
