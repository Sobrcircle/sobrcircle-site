Terms in plain words

No surprises after you sign.

  • Landlord Shield: month-to-month

    Cancel before your next monthly renewal and it ends at the end of the month you’ve paid for. A case engagement already accepted continues under its own terms.

  • Management plans: 90 days, then monthly

    Either side can end with 30 calendar days’ written notice, which can be given during the initial period, so the earliest ordinary end date is day 90.

  • Price hold

    Proposed: your monthly rates are held for 12 months, then any change comes with 60 days’ notice. Not a 12-month locked contract.

  • Vacant units

    The monthly fee continues while an enrolled unit is vacant, because your software, records and the plan’s services continue. Existing agreements keep their own vacancy terms.

  • Leaving

    When management ends we hand over records, balances and open matters properly. Nothing is simply dropped.

  • Existing clients

Pricing questions.

Is the price per unit?

No. The calculator shows a monthly total for your whole portfolio. The first tenancy has a first-unit rate and each additional tenancy costs less.

I own a condo in Toronto and another in Ottawa. Do I pay the first-unit rate twice?

No. The first-unit rate applies once per owner account across Ontario. Your second condo is priced at the additional rate.

Why a flat monthly fee instead of a percentage of rent?

Our plans are flat monthly prices, so your fee doesn’t rise when your rent does. As a share of rent it depends on the rent: $199 with the Legal Guarantee on a $2,500 rental is about 8%, and that includes our paralegals’ fees on eligible new LTB matters, which most managers bill separately. We don’t bill a percentage, and we don’t claim to be the lowest price.

Why can’t I price a house and a condo together here?

Mixed portfolios and 11 or more tenancies get a written quote, so we can look at the properties, locations and any local needs together. Book a call and we’ll prepare it.

Are site visits included?

No, visits aren’t part of the monthly fee. You can book one-off visits on any plan, and with Property Management or Property Management + Legal Guarantee you can add Planned Local Care for regular attendance. Everything is agreed before anyone attends.

Is LandlordEzy an extra cost?

No. LandlordEzy is included with every plan. It’s the system we use to run your rental, and you get your own owner portal.

Do you pay my condo fees?

No. Condo fee payment management isn’t part of any plan.

Tell us your properties and cities. We’ll confirm the monthly total and anything local in writing.
