N4 and N1, included

From missed rent to a served N4.

LandlordEzy fills the official N4 from your rent ledger and works out the arrears, so you can prepare one in around 60 seconds when your ledger is accurate. No OLH drafting fee for notices you prepare yourself.

  1. Rent is missedYou get a rent-unpaid alert, and the arrears are already in your ledger.
  2. Review the auto-filled N4Periods, amounts and the termination date come from your ledger. Check every detail before you sign.
  3. E-sign and serveServe by a method the rules allow; email only with the tenant’s written consent. LandlordEzy generates the Certificate of Service.
  4. Filing remindersReminders around the termination and filing dates, so the next step isn’t missed.
  5. Optional: the L1 and the hearingIf the tenant doesn’t pay, ask for our licensed paralegals at the member case rate from $995 + HST and disbursements, if eligible. No upgrade required.

Questions about Shield.

Who is Landlord Shield for?

Owners who look after their own tenants and want proper tools and a team behind them. Tenant communication, maintenance and arrears decisions stay with you.

Does Landlord Shield collect rent for me?

Your tenant pays rent directly into your own bank account by Interac e-Transfer, so timing is set by your bank and the payment. LandlordEzy matches deposits to your ledger and our team checks the records. Payments made another way are recorded from records you provide.

Is Equifax rent reporting included?

Yes, for enrolled tenancies, with no extra reporting subscription. On-time rent can help tenants build credit history, and unpaid rent may be reported as a delinquency where the program permits, subject to its consent, accuracy and verification rules. It’s different from screening credit checks, which cost from $29.99 per report.

I’m on Property Management. Can I move to Landlord Shield instead of leaving?

Yes. A plan change isn’t automatic: we agree it with you in writing, including who your tenants contact from then on, which day-to-day responsibilities pass to you, and how any open matter is completed or handed over. Your software, records and rent ledger stay with you.

What if I only want the software?

LandlordEzy can be used on its own, without our monthly ledger help. See LandlordEzy plans. With Landlord Shield the software for your enrolled tenancies is already included, so there’s no second software bill.
