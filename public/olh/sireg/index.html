SIREG and Simple Investor owners

One place to start, for affected unit owners.

For owners of units managed by SIREG Management or The Simple Investor, and their tenants. The live Owner Centre holds the free routes, the dated updates and the official sources. This page points you to each one.

General information, not legal advice.

Independent. OLH is not affiliated with or endorsed by SIREG Management Inc., The Simple Investor, any related company, any insolvency professional, any condominium corporation or board, or their legal counsel.

Your decisions are your own. Everything here is general information. You decide whether and how to use it. OLH does not tell any owner or tenant to cancel or end an agreement, to change where rent is paid, or to hire OLH, and does not give legal advice.

Our role

What OLH does, and what it doesn’t.

OLH does

  • Shares information and resources that owners can use without hiring OLH.
  • Runs the free Owner Registry and the Condominium Owner Initiative.
  • Publishes dated updates and keeps the record: replaced updates stay up, labelled.
  • Offers property management to owners who choose it, on a written agreement.
  • Collects rent for a unit only after that owner’s file is cleared and OLH’s authority for the unit is confirmed.

OLH does not

  • Give legal advice, including about your SIREG or Simple Investor agreement.
  • Tell any owner to end an agreement, or say that anyone’s agreement has ended.
  • Tell any tenant to change how or where rent is paid.
  • Require you to hire OLH to register, join a group or use the free routes.
  • Speak for SIREG Management, The Simple Investor, any insolvency professional or any condominium corporation.

Joining OLH is optional.

Signing up is your voluntary decision. OLH does not advise you to end another agreement. Get independent legal advice about your existing agreement before you change anything. Registering, owner groups and general information never require hiring OLH.

Owners who have already signed up keep the terms of their own agreement. For OLH’s current plans and prices, see pricing.

Need a lawyer for your own agreement? The Law Society Referral Service can help you find one. Corrections to anything we publish: info@olhgroup.ca.

Questions.

Do I have to hire OLH to use the registry, the initiative or the updates?

No. Registering, owner groups, the updates, the videos and Connect My Unit are free and don’t require hiring OLH. Registering is not a retainer, a proxy, a vote or a payment, and it doesn’t make anyone an OLH client.

Is any of this legal advice?

No. Everything on these pages is general information. For advice about your own agreement or situation, speak with your own lawyer. The Law Society Referral Service can help you find one.

I rent one of these units. Should I change where I pay rent?

Don’t change how or where you pay rent because of a public post, a video or a group comment. Act only on verified written information for your own unit. OLH does not tell any tenant to change where rent is paid, and a match on Connect My Unit is not permission for anyone to collect rent.

I already signed up with OLH. Which terms apply to me?

The terms of the agreement you signed. Existing owner arrangements keep their own terms. For OLH’s current plans and prices, see pricing.

Where is the latest news?

In the dated updates on the live Owner Centre, newest first, with official sources. We keep it there so there is one record, and we don’t repeat it on this page.

Questions about your unit?

The live Owner Centre has the next steps, the dated updates and the owner Q&A. If you’d like to talk to our team, a 15-minute call is free and creates no obligation.
