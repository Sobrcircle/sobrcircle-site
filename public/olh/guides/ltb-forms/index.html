Guide · LTB forms and notices

LTB forms and notices, in plain words.

Which notice to serve, which application follows it, and the numbers that matter. Every Landlord and Tenant Board form Ontario landlords use most, on one page.

Book a 15-minute callWhich form do I need?

Ending or enforcing a tenancy almost always starts with a notice, an N form, served on the tenant. If the tenant doesn’t pay, fix the problem or leave by the date on it, you apply to the Board with the matching L form, and a hearing is scheduled. A tenant can apply too, with a T form. Only the Board can order an eviction, and only the Sheriff enforces it.

  1. N formsNotices you serveEach has its own grounds, notice period and rules. Served on the tenant first.
  2. L formsApplications you fileFiled with the Board after the notice period. This is where the $186 filing fee and the hearing come in.
  3. T formsApplications a tenant filesAbout rights, repairs or a notice given in bad faith. The response is documentation.

Which form do I need?

Start with what’s happening.

Pick the situation. Each path shows the notice, then the application that follows it. Tap a form to jump to its rules.

  • N4L1

    Rent is owed now

    Serve the N4 as soon as rent is late. If it isn’t paid in full by the termination date, file the L1 the next day.

  • L9

    You want payment, not an eviction

    The L9 asks for a money order while the tenancy continues. No notice is needed first.

  • N4N8L2

    Rent is paid, but always late

    Serve an N4 each late month to build the record. Then an N8 ending at the end of a period, then the L2.

  • N5L2

    Noise, damage or too many occupants

    On a first N5 the tenant has 7 days to correct the problem. If they don’t, file the L2.

  • N7L2

    Serious safety problems or serious damage

    The N7 gives 10 days’ notice and no chance to correct.

  • N6L2

    An illegal act or illegal business

    The N6 has no correction window. Check the current form for the notice period.

  • N12L2

    You, a close family member or a buyer will move in

    60 days’ notice with one month’s compensation, or 120 days or more with none for your own or a family member’s use. A buyer’s N12 always owes compensation. You can file the L2 right after serving.

  • N13L2

    Work that needs a permit and an empty unit

    120 days’ notice, and compensation. You can file the L2 right after serving.

  • L5

    Recovering the capital cost

    Eligible capital work may support an above-guideline increase afterwards.

  • N11L3

    A signed agreement, or the tenant’s own notice

    The tenant signed an N11 or gave an N9, then stayed past the date. Most L3s are decided without a hearing.

  • L4

    A settlement or conditional order was broken

    File the L4 within 30 days of the breach.

  • N1

    A guideline increase

    At least 90 days’ written notice, once every 12 months.

  • N2

    A unit exempt from the guideline

    Typically one first occupied after November 15, 2018. The same 90-day and 12-month rules apply.

  • L5

    More than the guideline

    Needs the Board’s approval.

  • T2

    About rights: entry, locks, services, harassment

    The response is documentation: entry notices, messages and repair records.

  • T5

    Bad faith after an N12 or N13

    The response is proof of genuine intent.

Does the wrong form matter? Yes. Filing the wrong notice or application is one of the most common reasons eviction cases are delayed or dismissed at the LTB.

N forms

Notices you serve.

What each notice is for, the notice period and other rules, and the application that comes next. Forms marked 2026/09 were reissued after Bill 60.

FormWhat it’s forThe rules that matterWhat comes next
N1Notice of Rent IncreaseFull N1 guide · Official formRaising the rent by up to the annual guideline.
  • At least 90 days’ written notice before the increase takes effect.
  • Once every 12 months, and not in a tenant’s first 12 months.
  • No more than the guideline unless the LTB approves an above-guideline increase (L5).
Nothing to file. The new rent starts on the effective date. See the rent increases guide.
N2Notice of Rent Increase (Unit Partially Exempt)Full N2 guide · Official formRaising the rent on a unit exempt from the guideline, typically one first occupied after November 15, 2018.
  • At least 90 days’ written notice is still required.
  • Once every 12 months.
  • No guideline cap applies.
Nothing to file. See the rent increases guide.
N42026/09Notice to End a Tenancy Early for Non-Payment of RentFull N4 guide · Official formRent that is owed and overdue, in full or in part.
  • Termination date at least 7 days after service for any tenancy type, for N4s served on or after September 21, 2026 (12 days by regular mail, 8 by courier).
  • The tenant voids it by paying all rent owing on or before the termination date.
  • Only rent goes on it: no late fees, NSF charges or damages.
  • Use the 2026/09 version of the form.
Not paid in full: file an L1 the day after the termination date. Want payment without ending the tenancy: an L9.
N52026/09Notice to End Tenancy for Interfering with Others, Damage or OvercrowdingFull N5 guide · Official formSubstantial interference with others’ reasonable enjoyment, wilful or negligent damage, or more occupants than standards permit.
  • First N5: termination date at least 20 days after service. The tenant has 7 days to correct the problem and void it.
  • Second N5 within 6 months (and at least 7 days after the first): at least 14 days, with no chance to correct.
  • Describe what the tenant did and what they must do to correct it.
Not corrected, or a second N5: file an L2 no later than 30 days after the termination date.
N62026/09Notice to End Tenancy for Illegal Acts or Misrepresenting IncomeFull N6 guide · Official formAn illegal act or illegal business at the property, or misrepresented income in rent-geared-to-income housing.
  • No correction window: the tenant can’t void it by stopping the behaviour.
  • Notice period: check the current N6 on the LTB site before you set the date.
  • Police reports, charges and witness statements carry weight.
File an L2 no later than 30 days after the termination date.
N7Notice to End Tenancy for Serious Problems in the Rental Unit or ComplexFull N7 guide · Official formSeriously impaired safety, serious damage, or serious interference in a building of 3 units or fewer where the landlord also lives.
  • Termination date at least 10 days after service.
  • No correction window.
  • “Serious” is a real threshold. Ordinary disturbance or damage is an N5.
File an L2 no later than 30 days after the termination date.
N82026/09Notice to End Tenancy at the End of the Term (Persistent Late Payment)Full N8 guide · Official formA tenant who always pays, but pays late.
  • At least 60 days’ notice. The termination date must be the last day of a rental period or the term.
  • Since September 21, 2026: rent received more than 7 days late at least 3 times in any 6-month period. Payments applied to older arrears don’t count as late.
  • No correction window, but the tenant can argue the lateness wasn’t persistent. Keep due dates and paid dates.
File an L2 after the notice, no later than 30 days after the termination date.
N11Agreement to End the TenancyFull N11 guide · Official formLandlord and tenant both agree to end the tenancy on a set date.
  • Both sign voluntarily. An N11 signed at lease signing as a condition of renting is generally unenforceable.
  • Any move-out date both agree on is valid.
  • Put any agreed terms, such as a payment to move, in writing.
The tenant stays past the date: file an L3 (no hearing in most cases).
N122026/09Notice to End Tenancy: Landlord, Purchaser or Family Member Requires the UnitFull N12 guide · Official formThe landlord, a purchaser, or a close family member (spouse, child, parent, or a spouse’s child or parent) will live in the unit for at least one year.
  • At least 60 days’ notice, ending on the last day of a rental period or the term.
  • One month’s rent compensation, or another acceptable unit, by the termination date. Since September 21, 2026, none is owed on an own-use or family N12 giving 120 or more days. A purchaser’s N12 always owes it.
  • Whoever moves in must do so within 60 days of the termination date, or bad faith is presumed on a T5.
File an L2, immediately after service if you like, with an affidavit or declaration of good-faith intent to occupy.
N132026/09Notice to End Tenancy: Demolition, Conversion or Extensive RepairsFull N13 guide · Official formDemolition, conversion to another use, or repairs that need a building permit and a vacant unit. Cosmetic work doesn’t qualify.
  • At least 120 days’ notice, ending on the last day of a rental period or the term.
  • Compensation is required. The amount depends on the ground and the building: check the current N13 on the LTB site.
  • Repairs or conversion: a tenant who asks in writing can move back in. Since September 21, 2026 you give a written estimated completion date, updates, a completion notice and 60 days to reoccupy.
File an L2, which can be filed immediately after service. Eligible capital costs may later support an L5.

L and T forms

Applications to the Board.

Landlords file L forms after the notice period, with the filing fee. Tenants file T forms. Either way, a hearing usually follows.

Landlord applications

FormWhat it’s forThe rules that matterWhat comes next
L12026/09Application to Evict a Tenant for Non-Payment of Rent and Collect Rent OwingFull L1 guide · Official formAfter an N4 whose termination date passed without payment in full.
  • File the day after the N4 termination date, not before.
  • Package: the L1, a copy of the N4, the Certificate of Service and the $186 filing fee.
  • The order can include arrears to the hearing date, the filing fee and daily compensation.
  • L1s filed on or after September 21, 2026: a tenant who wants to raise their own issues must first pay at least half the arrears claimed (see Bill 60).
The Board schedules a hearing. Only the Sheriff enforces an eviction order.
L22026/09Application to End a Tenancy and Evict a Tenant or Collect MoneyFull L2 guide · Official formAfter an N5, N6, N7, N8, N12 or N13, and for abandonment and superintendent-unit evictions.
  • No later than 30 days after the notice’s termination date. After an N12 or N13 you can file immediately after service.
  • Package: the L2, a copy of the notice, the Certificate of Service and the $186 filing fee.
  • Can also claim compensation for damage or other money owed.
A hearing. For an N12, bring the declaration and proof of compensation.
L3Application to End a Tenancy: Tenant Gave Notice or Agreed to TerminateFull L3 guide · Official formThe tenant gave notice (N9) or signed an N11, then didn’t leave.
  • File before the termination date or within 30 days after it.
  • $186 filing fee.
Most are decided by written order without a hearing.
L4Application to End a Tenancy: Tenant Failed to Meet Settlement ConditionsFull L4 guide · Official formThe tenant broke the conditions of a mediated settlement or a conditional LTB order.
  • File within 30 days of the breach.
  • Record the breach exactly: dates, amounts, messages.
  • $186 filing fee.
Usually decided ex parte, without a new full hearing.
L5Application for an Above Guideline Rent Increase (AGI)Full L5 guide · Official formMajor capital work (roof, boiler or mechanical, windows, elevators, life-safety systems) or extraordinary cost increases.
  • At most 3% above the guideline a year, spread over up to 3 years if the justified increase is larger.
  • Invoices, contracts, permits and proof of payment for every item.
  • Filing fee: check the current fee on the LTB site.
Since July 1, 2026, serve the documents within 7 days of the Board’s direction and file a certificate of service within 5 days.
L92026/09Application to Collect Rent the Tenant Owes (Without Eviction)Full L9 guide · Official formA money order for arrears while the tenancy continues.
  • No termination notice needed.
  • File while the tenant still lives in the unit. After move-out, it’s an L10.
  • $186 filing fee.
The order is enforceable like a court judgment.

Filing checklist

  • The completed application form
  • A copy of the notice you served
  • The Certificate of Service, showing how and when you served it
  • The $186 LTB filing fee (L1, L2, L3, L4 and L9)
  • Your evidence: the rent ledger, photos, messages, inspection reports

After the hearing

A request to review an LTB order must be filed within 15 days of the order. Only the Sheriff can carry out an eviction order: changing the locks, removing belongings or shutting off utilities yourself is illegal, even after an order.

Tenant applications

When a tenant files against you, the response is documentation.

FormWhat it’s forThe rules that matterWhat comes next
T2Tenant Application About RightsFull T2 guide · Official formA tenant alleges illegal entry, changed locks, substantial interference, withheld vital services, harassment, or disposal of property.
  • The response is documentation: entry notices, communication logs, repair records and witness accounts.
  • Context matters. Record why every entry happened.
A hearing. The tenant may seek a rent abatement, fines or moving costs.
T52026/09Tenant Application: Notice Given in Bad FaithFull T5 guide · Official formA former tenant alleges an N12 or N13 was given in bad faith.
  • After a renovation N13, the deadline is the later of 2 years after move-out or 6 months after the work finishes. For an N12, check the current T5 on the LTB site.
  • Penalties can include up to a year of rent difference, moving and storage costs, and administrative fines.
  • The response is proof of genuine intent: occupancy evidence, utility records, timelines, and documented changes of circumstance.
A hearing. The best protection starts before the N12 or N13 is served.

Serving a notice

Serve it right, or start over.

Invalid service is one of the most common reasons notices fail at the LTB. These are the approved methods, with the days to count for an N4.

MethodCounts as servedN4: days to count
Handed to the tenantThe day it’s handed over7 days
Handed to another adult in the unitSame day7 days
Placed in the mailbox or mail slotThe day it’s left where mail is normally delivered7 days
Slid under the doorSame as the mailbox7 days
CourierThe first business day after sending8 days in total
Regular mail5 days after mailing12 days in total
Email, with the tenant’s written consent to email serviceThe day it’s sent7 days
Tribunals Ontario Portal, with consentThe day it’s uploaded7 days

Not valid service

  • Taping it to the door
  • Leaving it with a neighbour
  • Posting it in the building
  • Text message
  • Voicemail
  • A phone call

Invalid service means an invalid notice, and starting over. Fill in a Certificate of Service the day you serve.

A worked N4 example

You email the N4, with the tenant’s written consent to email service, on December 6. Don’t count the day you serve. The earliest termination date is December 13. If the rent owing isn’t paid in full by then, file the L1 on December 14.

A one-day error voids the notice.

The same methods apply to every N form; the day counts above are for the N4. By regular mail, add 5 days; by courier, add 1.

Questions about LTB forms.

What’s the difference between an N form, an L form and a T form?

An N form is a notice you serve on the tenant first. If the tenant doesn’t pay, fix the problem or leave, you file the matching L form, an application to the Landlord and Tenant Board, and a hearing is scheduled. T forms are applications a tenant files. Only the Board can order an eviction, and only the Sheriff enforces it.

Does using the wrong form matter?

Yes. Filing the wrong notice or application is one of the most common reasons eviction cases are delayed or dismissed at the LTB. A defective notice or bad service means serving again and restarting the clock.

Can I file the L1 before the N4’s termination date?

No. An L1 filed before the termination date has passed is rejected. If the tenant hasn’t paid in full, file the day after.

Can I put late fees on an N4?

No. Only unpaid rent goes on an N4. Late fees, NSF charges and damages don’t belong on it, and adding them can make the notice invalid.

Can I serve a notice by text message?

No. Text messages, voicemail, phone calls, taping the notice to the door, leaving it with a neighbour and posting it in the building are not valid service. Email works only where the tenant has given written consent to email service. Fill in a Certificate of Service the day you serve.

When can I file an L2 after an N12?

Right after you serve the N12. The same applies to an N13. For an N5, N6, N7 or N8, file no later than 30 days after the termination date.

How much is the LTB filing fee?

$186 per application for an L1, L2, L3, L4 or L9. For an L5, check the current fee on the LTB site. The LTB also has a Fee Waiver Request form.

Where do I get the official forms?

From the LTB’s own forms page at tribunalsontario.ca/ltb/forms. Eleven forms were reissued as version 2026/09 after Bill 60. On Landlord Shield, LandlordEzy fills the N4 from your rent ledger and the N1 from your lease.
