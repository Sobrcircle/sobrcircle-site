Landlord Shield and Property Management include advice every month and a member price for a qualifying new case. Property Management + Legal Guarantee adds eligible new LTB case work with no activation fee.

Who qualifies for the member case rate
  • The facts behind the problem first arise after the affected unit’s enrollment date (the date your paid Shield or Management membership is accepted with that unit enrolled) and membership has been continuous since. Each unit has its own date.
  • An older problem can’t qualify later: a new notice, a filing date, waiting, or leaving and rejoining doesn’t change when it started.
  • When the unit was enrolled there were no existing arrears or related dispute, as disclosed at joining.
  • One member case per owner account in any rolling 12 months, measured from the start of the previous accepted member case. The clock follows the owner account: cancelling, rejoining or adding units never resets it.
  • After a gap in membership, a unit’s date is its new accepted enrollment; moving between our plans while continuously enrolled doesn’t change it.
  • A problem that began before the unit’s enrollment date is quoted at the standard rate (from $1,495).
  • Legal Guarantee matters are covered from your accepted service start; they never use up or trigger the member case.
  • Every case is subject to an eligibility check, a conflict check and a separate written engagement before work starts. The member case is a paid, fixed-scope engagement, not a temporary Legal Guarantee.

Legal questions.

What is member legal access?

A member benefit of Landlord Shield and Property Management: one 15-minute consultation a month with a licensed paralegal, and a member rate (from $995 + HST and disbursements) for a qualifying new non-payment case through one hearing. One per owner account in any rolling 12 months, for problems that first arise after the unit is enrolled. It isn’t a separate plan, and the member case is a paid engagement, not the Legal Guarantee.

I already have a problem. Can I join and use the member rate?

No. The member rate is for problems whose facts first arise after a unit’s enrollment date. There’s no waiting period, but a problem that already exists doesn’t qualify by joining, waiting or rejoining. It’s handled as a standalone engagement from $1,495 + HST and disbursements, and you can still join for the future.

Why choose the Legal Guarantee if there’s a member case rate?

The Legal Guarantee covers professional work on eligible new LTB matters across all your enrolled units while you’re enrolled, with no activation fee and no one-case limit. The member case rate is one discounted case per owner account in any rolling 12 months. It comes down to whether you’d rather pay when you need it, or have it arranged in advance.

Can I prepare an N4 myself?

Yes. LandlordEzy fills the official N4 from your ledger, so it can take around 60 seconds when your records are accurate. You review it, e-sign it and serve it by a permitted method, and LandlordEzy generates the Certificate of Service. There’s no OLH drafting fee for notices you prepare yourself.
