What we handle

Rent, tenants, repairs and notices.

Everything below is part of the monthly price. Where something isn’t included, we say so.

Rent and money

Rent collected, matched to your unit and paid out to you, with a statement every month.

  • Rent collection, with reminders and receipts for your tenants

  • Owner payouts started as soon as rent clears (see below)

Tenants

Maintenance

How you get paid

Paid when it clears, not on a fixed date.

How owner payouts work. OLH normally initiates an owner payout the same business day after the tenant payment has been received, identified, available as cleared funds, and matched and allocated to the authorized managed unit. For example, if November rent arrives on October 25, the payout is normally initiated once that payment has cleared and been matched, rather than waiting for the first of the month.

When you actually receive the funds depends on banking cut-offs and processing, weekends and holidays, correct remittance details, and any hold, reversal or review. Initiating a payout is not the same as the bank completing it.

Every payout and every expense shows on your monthly statement.

What counts as an emergency

No heat, flooding, lockouts, and anything threatening the property or tenant safety. Those go to the emergency line and don’t wait for office hours.

Straight answer

Who it isn’t for.

We manage residential tenancies under Ontario’s Residential Tenancies Act. If you need one of these, call someone else.

  • A live-in superintendent

    We don’t provide on-site staff living at the building.

  • Commercial or retail

    Offices, storefronts and other commercial space are outside what we do.

  • Short-term rentals

    Airbnb-style stays aren’t residential tenancies, so they’re not for us.

Managing it yourself? OLH Landlord Shield gives you the software, a monthly ledger check and a paralegal to talk to, from $59 a month. If we’re not the right fit, we’ll tell you on the first call.

Questions about management.

What does Property Management cost?

From $149 a month for your first tenancy and $79 for each additional tenancy, plus HST. It’s a flat monthly price, the same for any property type, and never a percentage of your rent. There’s no setup fee. Price my portfolio

How do my tenants reach you, and what about emergencies?

Tenants message us through their LandlordEzy portal, by email or by phone, and we handle messages during office hours, Monday to Friday. Emergencies such as no heat, flooding, a lockout, or anything threatening the property or someone’s safety go to our emergency line instead of waiting for the office to open.

Are inspections included?

No routine inspections are included in the monthly fee. When you want someone on site, we book a local visit at $75 + HST: up to 45 minutes, dated photos, a standard checklist and a report in your owner portal. Every visit is confirmed with you first. Visit prices

When do I get paid?

We normally start your payout the same business day the tenant’s payment has cleared and been matched to your unit, rather than holding it for a set date. When it reaches your account depends on your bank, weekends and holidays. You get a statement every month.

Do I approve repairs?

Yes. You set an approval limit. Routine jobs under it go ahead; anything over it comes to you with a price before work is ordered. Completed work is closed with photo or video proof in your owner portal, and the cost appears on your statement.

What happens if a tenant stops paying?

We follow up on arrears and run the N4 process. If a new non-payment problem goes to the Landlord and Tenant Board, a qualifying case is available at the member rate from $995 + HST and disbursements. On Property Management + Legal Guarantee, $50 a month more, our paralegals’ fees on eligible new LTB matters are included. Compare the legal options

Can you take over mid-tenancy, or with a dispute already open?

Yes. Most owners come to us with tenants already in place: same lease, same rent, same rights. If an N4, N5 or LTB file is already open, we check it for validity during the handover. Taking over an existing dispute is a separate engagement, quoted before any work starts.

Can I cancel?

Property Management starts with a 90-day initial period, then continues month-to-month. Either side can end it with 30 calendar days’ written notice, which can be given during the initial period. When management ends we hand over records, balances and open matters properly.
