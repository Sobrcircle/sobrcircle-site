Questions people ask

Where can I find AA meetings in Los Angeles tonight?

Open SobrCircle and the map shows meetings around Los Angeles with their times and live navigation. You can also check the local AA directory listed on this page or call 323-936-4343.

Are there NA and Cocaine Anonymous meetings in Los Angeles?

Yes. NA meetings in the area are listed by Southern California Region of Narcotics Anonymous, and CA meetings by Cocaine Anonymous Los Angeles (CALA). SobrCircle shows them alongside AA meetings on one map.

Do recovery meetings in Los Angeles cost anything?

No. Meetings are free to attend. Groups may pass a basket for voluntary contributions, and newcomers are never expected to give.

Can I go to a meeting if I just want to listen?

Yes. Look for an open meeting, which anyone can attend. You never have to speak, and you can leave whenever you like.
