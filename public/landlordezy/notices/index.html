Bill 60, built in

The rules changed on September 21. Your notices already know.

An N4 now needs only 7 days. Tenants who want to raise other issues at an arrears hearing must first pay you half the arrears. And older versions of ten LTB forms stop being accepted on November 30, 2026.

7 days

Minimum N4 notice for notices given on or after September 21, 2026.

50%

Of the arrears claimed, paid to you 7 days before the hearing if the tenant raises other issues.

Nov 30

Last day the LTB accepts the old N5, N6, N8, N12, N13, L1, L2, L9, L10 and T5.

Questions, answered.

Can I serve an N4 myself, without a paralegal?

Yes. Landlords can prepare and serve their own N4. LandlordEzy removes the arithmetic and date mistakes that most often get notices thrown out. For contested hearings, licensed paralegals can represent you.

How many days does an N4 give the tenant now?

For notices given on or after September 21, 2026, the termination date must be at least 7 days after you give the notice, not counting that day. Add one business day if you courier it and five days if you mail it.

Can the tenant cancel the N4?

Yes. If the tenant pays everything owing by the termination date, the notice is void.

What is the 2027 rent increase guideline?

1.9% for increases that take effect between January 1 and December 31, 2027. Units first occupied as residential units after November 15, 2018 are generally exempt from the guideline, but still need 90 days’ notice and 12 months between increases.
