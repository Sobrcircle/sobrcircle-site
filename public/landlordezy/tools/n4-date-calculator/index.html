How it’s calculated

For an N4 given on or after September 21, 2026, the termination date must be at least 7 days after you give the notice. Don’t count the day you give it.

  • In person, mailbox or under the door: the notice is given that day.
  • Courier: add one business day for delivery.
  • Mail or Xpresspost: add five days for delivery.

The tenant can void the notice by paying everything owing by the termination date. If they don’t, the earliest day to file an L1 is the day after the termination date. Notices given before September 21, 2026 used the old 14-day minimum.

Sources: LTB N4 instructions · Tribunals Ontario, Sept. 21, 2026 update

Common questions

Do I count the day I give the notice?

No. Count 7 days starting the day after you give the notice. Handed to the tenant on March 3, the earliest termination date is March 10.

What if I mail it?

Add five days for mail or Xpresspost before counting the 7 days. Mailed March 3, the earliest termination date is March 15. For courier, add one business day.

Can I tape the N4 to the door?

No. The LTB’s instructions say you cannot give an N4 by posting it on the door of the rental unit. Hand it over, leave it in the mailbox, slide it under the door, or send it by courier or mail.

When can I file the L1?

The earliest day to file is the day after the termination date, if the tenant hasn’t paid everything owing or moved out.
