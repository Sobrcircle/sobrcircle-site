Pricing questions

What counts as a door?

A door is a rentable unit: a house, an apartment, a basement suite. A duplex is two doors. Self Management is $9.99 a month plus $1 for each door, so it scales with your portfolio.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan needs no card. You only enter payment details when you upgrade.

Is HST included?

No. Prices are in Canadian dollars and 13% HST is added at checkout.

Can I change my door count or cancel?

Yes. Your price adjusts automatically as your door count changes, and you can cancel anytime.

Who pays for tenant screening?

You choose. Credit checks are $29.99 each, and with Tenant Pays the applicant covers it. You can credit the fee toward the first month’s rent if you approve them.

I’m already paying through PayPal. Does anything change?

No. Existing PayPal subscribers stay on their current plan and billing. New subscriptions are billed through Stripe in Canadian dollars.

Your first two tenants are free.

No credit card. Add your first property, invite a tenant, and let the software do the chasing.
