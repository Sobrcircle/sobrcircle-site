Questions, answered.

Does a credit check hurt the applicant’s score?

No. LandlordEzy credit checks are soft inquiries, which don’t affect credit scores, so applicants can apply to several places without penalty.

How much does screening cost?

Pre-screening is free. The AI credit check with a full Equifax report is $29.99, and you add only the checks each application needs. You or the applicant can pay.

What can a landlord ask an applicant for in Ontario?

Ontario’s Human Rights Code regulations let landlords ask for rental history, credit references and permission to run a credit check, and to consider income information together with those, not on its own. Decisions can’t be based on protected grounds, including receiving public assistance. This is general information, not legal advice.

Does it work outside Ontario?

Yes. Pre-screening and Equifax credit checks work in every province. Ontario-only checks are labelled as such.
