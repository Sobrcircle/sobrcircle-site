Ontario rent increases in 2027: the 1.9% guideline

Ontario’s rent increase guideline for 2027 is 1.9%, down from 2.1% in 2026. Here’s how much you can raise the rent, when, and how to give notice.

Reviewed October 15, 2026·5 min read·LandlordEzy team

The short answer

In 2027 you can raise most rents by up to 1.9%: once every 12 months, with at least 90 days’ written notice on Form N1. Units first occupied after November 15, 2018 are exempt from the guideline, but not from the notice and timing rules.

The 2027 guideline is 1.9%

The guideline applies to rent increases that take effect between January 1 and December 31, 2027. It’s based on Ontario’s Consumer Price Index and is the most a landlord can raise most tenants’ rent in a year without approval from the Landlord and Tenant Board.

The three rules

  1. Once every 12 months. Count from the last increase or, if there hasn’t been one, the day the tenancy began.
  2. At least 90 days’ written notice, on the LTB’s Form N1. A mailed notice counts as given five days after mailing.
  3. No more than the guideline for the year the increase takes effect, unless the unit is exempt or the Board approves more.

An example

The rent is $1,950 and the last increase took effect January 1, 2026. The next increase can take effect January 1, 2027, at up to 1.9%: a new rent of $1,987.05, or $37.05 more a month. Exempt units

The guideline doesn’t apply to units occupied for the first time for residential purposes after November 15, 2018. You can raise the rent on those units by more than the guideline, but the 90-day notice and 12-month rules still apply.

Going above the guideline

For units covered by the guideline, an increase above it needs an order from the Board on Form L5 (an above-guideline increase), for things like major capital work, a large jump in municipal taxes or new security services. A tenant can also agree to an above-guideline increase on Form N10 in exchange for capital work or a new service.

Don’t forget the deposit

Each year you owe interest on a last month’s rent deposit at the guideline rate: 1.9% for interest due in 2027. When the rent goes up, you can keep that interest to top the deposit up toward the new rent. The deposit interest calculator shows the split.
