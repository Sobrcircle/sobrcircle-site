Ontario landlord guide

Ontario LTB forms, explained

Every Landlord and Tenant Board form a landlord is likely to meet, what it’s for, and how it’s filed. Updated for the forms that changed on September 21, 2026.

Reviewed October 15, 2026·8 min read·LandlordEzy team

The short answer

Use the current forms: the N5, N6, N8, N12, N13, L1, L2, L9, L10 and T5 changed on September 21, 2026, and old versions stop being accepted on November 30, 2026. Notices go to the other side; applications are filed with the Board, most of them online through the Tribunals Ontario Portal.

Forms that changed on September 21, 2026

The N5, N6, N8, N12, N13, L1, L2, L9, L10 and T5 were updated with Bill 60. Previous versions stop being accepted on November 30, 2026. Always download the current form from Tribunals Ontario.

Notices a landlord gives

FormOfficial name and when it’s used
N1Notice of Rent IncreaseA standard rent increase, with at least 90 days’ written notice.
N2Notice of Rent Increase (Unit Partially Exempt)A rent increase for units that are partially exempt from the Act’s rent rules.
N3Notice to Increase the Rent and/or Charges for Care Services and MealsCare homes only.
N4Notice to End your Tenancy Early for Non-payment of RentThe tenant hasn’t paid rent that’s due. The first step to an L1.
N5Notice to End your Tenancy for Interfering with Others, Damage or OvercrowdingDamage, disturbing others or overcrowding.
N6Notice to End your Tenancy for Illegal Acts or Misrepresenting Income in a Rent-Geared-to-Income Rental UnitAn illegal act in the unit or complex, or misrepresented income in RGI housing.
N7Notice to End your Tenancy for Causing Serious Problems in the Rental Unit or Residential ComplexSerious problems, such as wilful damage or impaired safety.
N8Notice to End your Tenancy at the End of the TermEnding a tenancy at the end of the term, most often for persistent late payment.
N10Agreement to Increase the Rent Above the GuidelineA tenant agrees to an above-guideline increase for capital work or a new service.
N11Agreement to End the TenancyLandlord and tenant both agree to end the tenancy on a date.
N12Notice to End your Tenancy Because the Landlord, a Purchaser or a Family Member Requires the Rental UnitYou, a family member or a buyer will live in the unit.
N13Notice to End your Tenancy Because the Landlord Wants to Demolish the Rental Unit, Repair it or Convert it to Another UseDemolition, major repairs or conversion to another use.
N14Landlord’s Notice to the Spouse of the Tenant who Vacated the Rental UnitThe tenant has left and their spouse is still living in the unit.

Notices a tenant gives

FormOfficial name and when it’s used
N9Tenant’s Notice to End the TenancyA tenant gives notice that they’re moving out.
N15Tenant’s Notice to End my Tenancy Because of Fear of Sexual or Domestic Violence and AbuseA tenant ends the tenancy early for their safety.

Landlord applications

FormOfficial name and when it’s usedHow it’s filed
L1Application to evict a tenant for non-payment of rent and to collect rent the tenant owesAfter an N4 expires unpaid.Tribunals Ontario Portal
L2Application to End a Tenancy and Evict a Tenant or Collect MoneyAfter most other notices (N5, N6, N7, N8, N12, N13) or to collect other money.Tribunals Ontario Portal
L3Application to End a Tenancy – Tenant Gave Notice or Agreed to Terminate the TenancyThe tenant gave an N9 or signed an N11 but didn’t leave.Tribunals Ontario Portal
L4Application to End a Tenancy and Evict a Tenant – Tenant Failed to Meet Conditions of a Settlement or OrderThe tenant broke the terms of a settlement or order.Tribunals Ontario Portal
L5Application for a Rent Increase Above the GuidelineCapital work, large tax increases or new security costs.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email
L6Application for Review of a Work Order about Provincial Maintenance StandardsDisputing a provincial maintenance work order.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email
L7Application to Transfer a Care Home TenantCare homes only.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email
L8Application Because the Tenant Changed the LocksThe tenant changed the locks without consent.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email
L9Application to Collect Rent the Tenant OwesCollect rent without ending the tenancy (tenant still living there).Tribunals Ontario Portal
L10Application to Collect Money a Former Tenant OwesThe tenant has moved out and still owes money.Tribunals Ontario Portal

Tenant applications

FormOfficial name and when it’s usedHow it’s filed
T1Tenant Application for a RebateMoney the tenant says the landlord owes them.Tribunals Ontario Portal
T2Application about Tenant RightsHarassment, illegal entry, withheld services and similar issues.Tribunals Ontario Portal
T3Tenant Application for a Rent ReductionFor example, after a cut in services or municipal taxes.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email
T4Tenant Application – Landlord did not Comply with an Agreement to Increase Rent Above the GuidelineThe landlord didn’t do what an N10 promised.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email
T5Tenant Application – Landlord Gave a Notice of Termination in Bad FaithTypically after an N12 or N13.Tribunals Ontario Portal
T6Tenant Application about MaintenanceRepairs and maintenance.Tribunals Ontario Portal
T7Tenant Application about Suite MetersSuite-metered utilities.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email

Applications either side can file

FormOfficial name and when it’s usedHow it’s filed
A1Application about Whether the Act AppliesEither side asks whether the Residential Tenancies Act applies.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email
A2Application about a Sublet or an AssignmentDisputes about subletting or assigning the unit.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email
A4Application to Vary the Amount of a Rent ReductionChanging a rent reduction amount.Mail, courier, ServiceOntario or email

Before you file

  • Notices are given to the other side; applications are filed with the Board.
  • When you apply after a notice, you file a Certificate of Service showing when and how you gave it.
  • Applications marked “Tribunals Ontario Portal” are filed online through the Board’s portal.
