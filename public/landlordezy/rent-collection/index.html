How it works

Set it up once. Then stop chasing.

  1. 1

    Add one forwarding rule.

    Forward your bank’s Interac notification emails to your private LandlordEzy address. Your e-Transfer address, your bank and your tenants’ habits stay exactly the same.

  2. 2

    Match once.

    Match a payment to a tenant one time and LandlordEzy learns the payer, so next month’s rent records itself.

  3. 3

    Receipts and arrears, handled.

    Each payment lands on the oldest month owing, the tenant gets a receipt, and anyone behind shows up on your arrears list.

Questions, answered.

Do my tenants need to download an app?

No. They pay by Interac e-Transfer as they do today. If they like, they can see their rent history and receipts in a passwordless tenant portal.

Does LandlordEzy ever hold my money?

No. The money moves from Interac to your bank. LandlordEzy only reads a copy of the notification email to update your ledger.

What happens with a partial payment?

It’s applied to the oldest month owing, and the rest stays on the ledger as arrears, ready for a reminder or an N4.

What does it cost?

Automatic e-Transfer matching is part of Self Management: $9.99 a month plus $1 per door, plus HST. The Free plan includes rent tracking for up to two tenants.
