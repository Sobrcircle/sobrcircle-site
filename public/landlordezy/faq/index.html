Getting started

Is LandlordEzy free?

Yes. The Free plan covers up to two tenants with rent tracking, reminders, receipts, a tenant portal, pre-screening and free listings, with no credit card.

Is it only for Ontario?

The legal tools are built for Ontario’s Residential Tenancies Act and the LTB. Screening, Equifax credit checks and free listings work across Canada.

Do I need to install an app?

No. LandlordEzy works in any browser and can be added to your phone’s home screen like an app, without an app store.

Can I try it before signing up?

Yes. The live demo builds a private workspace shaped like your portfolio in about a minute, with no sales call.

Pricing and billing

How much does Self Management cost?

$9.99 a month plus $1 per door, plus HST. It scales up or down automatically with your door count.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts.

What about property management companies?

PM Free costs nothing. PM Manage is $29.99 a month plus $1 per door, and PM Growth is $129 a month plus $1 per door.

Rent and reporting

How does automatic rent collection work?

You forward your bank’s Interac notification emails to a private LandlordEzy address. Each payment is matched to the tenant and month, receipted and reconciled. Your money never leaves your bank.

Does reporting rent to Equifax need the tenant’s consent?

Reporting on-time rent does. Reporting unpaid rent as a debt doesn’t. LandlordEzy captures and records consent for you.

Screening

Does a credit check affect the applicant’s score?

No. Checks through LandlordEzy are soft inquiries.

Who pays for the credit check?

You choose. Checks start at $29.99, and with Tenant Pays the applicant covers it.

Notices and legal help

Are notices up to date with Bill 60?

Yes. Notices follow the rules in force on the day you serve, including the 7-day N4 and the updated forms.

Is LandlordEzy a law firm?

No. LandlordEzy is a technology platform. Legal services are provided by Landlord Pro Legal SPC, a licensed Ontario paralegal firm.

Can I talk to a paralegal?

Yes. Book a free 15-minute strategy call with the licensed paralegal team.

Privacy and security

Do you sell personal information?

No. LandlordEzy doesn’t sell personal information.

What happens to ID images used for verification?

For the Landlord Reference Network, ID images are deleted as soon as the team finishes verifying them.

Does LandlordEzy publish LTB orders?

No. LandlordEzy no longer publishes Landlord and Tenant Board orders. The Board publishes its own catalogue on Ontario’s open data portal.
