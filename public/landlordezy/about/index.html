About LandlordEzy

Built in Ontario, by a landlord, for landlords.

LandlordEzy brings everything an Ontario landlord juggles into one login: rent, screening, leases, Equifax reporting and LTB notices, with licensed people behind the software.

“We need each other.”

PR

Paul J. Rouillard

Founder, LandlordEzy · Broker of Record, List It Realty

Our story

Twenty-three years in the business. Fifteen years building the fix.

Paul J. Rouillard collected his first rent at 14, helping his parents run their properties. At 22 he started his own property management company, and he has spent the years since in Ontario rentals: leasing, collections, arrears, inspections, maintenance and hearings at the Landlord and Tenant Board.

He watched the system get harder for everyone. Good landlords were paying for tools that each did one thing, and for paperwork mistakes that cost them months. So he started building the platform he wished he’d had, and kept building it for fifteen years.

The idea behind it is simple: landlords need good tenants and tenants need good landlords. LandlordEzy gives both sides the tools, the records and the protection to do this the right way.
