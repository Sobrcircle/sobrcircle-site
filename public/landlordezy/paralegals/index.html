CaseBuilder for paralegals

The LTB research team you never had.

Describe the file in plain language, or upload the notice, and CaseBuilder ranks comparable Ontario Landlord and Tenant Board decisions, wins and losses, with the deciding factors. Then it builds the hearing pack.

Try CaseBuilder freeHearing prep & disclosure

Free to start · Research tool, not legal advice

How it works

From the client’s facts to a hearing pack.

  1. 1

    Describe it or upload it.

    Paste what happened, or drop in the N5, L2 or notice as a PDF or photo.

  2. 2

    See how the Board decided.

    Comparable decisions are ranked by similarity, with outcomes and the reasons that decided them.

  3. 3

    Walk in prepared.

    Generate a page-numbered hearing pack and a one-click disclosure bundle with tabbed exhibits.

Everything for an LTB practice.

Plain-language search

Search the Board’s published decisions by issue, notice type, RTA section and outcome.

Ranked comparables

The closest decisions first, wins and losses, so you see what beats a case like yours.

AI comparative analysis

Deciding factors, the evidence to bring, and examination and cross questions.

Disclosure bundle

A numbered exhibit list with every exhibit behind its own tab page, served-ready.

Matters

Organise research, analyses and documents by client matter.

Law library

The RTA, regulations, SPPA and Human Rights Code, cross-linked to forms.

CaseBuilder researches by situation, never by person. Orders removed at a party’s request stay excluded, and every result links back to the Board’s published decision.
