How it works

Reporting that runs itself.

  1. 1

    Your tenant opts in.

    Tenants consent once through a secure, no-login link, or when they sign the lease. Consent stays on file until they withdraw it.

  2. 2

    Your ledger does the work.

    LandlordEzy builds the monthly Equifax file from the rent you already track. No double entry.

  3. 3

    Credit builds, month by month.

    Each on-time payment strengthens your tenant’s credit file and their Rental Report Card.

Good for tenants. Good for you.

Good tenants build credit

For Canadians with thin credit files, a record of on-time rent can be the strongest proof they’re reliable.

A reason to pay on the first

When rent counts toward credit, tenants make it a priority.

Consent, handled and versioned

On-time reporting needs the tenant’s consent. LandlordEzy records it, and asks again if the wording ever changes.

Arrears, on the record

Unpaid rent is a debt. Positive reporting needs consent; reporting an unpaid debt does not. Keep the ledger accurate and the record stays clean.

Rental Report Card

Every on-time month builds a tenant’s verifiable rental record that they own and can share with their next landlord.

Monthly, automatically

Submissions go to Equifax every month from your ledger. You keep the ledger current; LandlordEzy handles the file.

Included for all your tenants in Self Management, from $9.99 a month plus $1 per door. See pricing

What your tenant sees

A rent record they’re proud of.

Tenants see every receipt, how to pay, and their on-time streak in a passwordless portal. The Rental Report Card they build with you goes with them to their next home.

  • Consent captured once, by a no-login link or at lease signing
  • Positive reporting held as “consent pending” until they agree
  • Re-consent requested automatically if the wording changes

Questions, answered.

Does the tenant have to agree?

For on-time (positive) reporting, yes. Consent is captured once and stays active until the tenant withdraws it. Reporting unpaid rent as a debt doesn’t require consent.

Which credit bureau do you report to?

Equifax Canada, every month.

Can late payments hurt a tenant’s credit?

Yes. On-time payments help build credit; late or missed payments may lower it. That’s exactly why tenants pay attention.
