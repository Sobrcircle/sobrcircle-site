Maintenance & compliance

Repairs, dispatched in one tap.

A tenant reports a leak. You tick three boxes: ask for their availability, get the owner’s approval, assign a vendor. LandlordEzy notifies everyone with one shared link, tracks photos and costs, and posts the bill.

How it works

Fifteen phone calls, down to five taps.

  1. 1

    The tenant reports it.

    Requests arrive with photos, straight from the tenant portal.

  2. 2

    One tap dispatches it.

    Tenant, owner and vendor are notified together and share one live link. Vendors don’t need an account.

  3. 3

    The bill files itself.

    Costs and photos are tracked, and the bill posts to the owner statement or your expense tracker.

The busywork of owning rentals, handled.

Vendors you can rank

Rate vendors after each job. Bad ones go on a do-not-call list and quietly disappear from your picks.

Fire safety

Compliance, from your pocket.

Walk the Ontario Fire Code checklist room by room, photograph each alarm, extinguisher and exit, and generate a signed report on the spot. Reminders make sure the next one happens on time.

Questions, answered.

Do vendors need a LandlordEzy account?

No. They get a text with a live link to the job, and that’s it.

What does the fire safety inspection cover?

The common Ontario Fire Code items for rental units: smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, extinguishers, exits and more, with a photo for each item and a PDF report you can keep on file.

Can I export expenses for my accountant?

Yes. Export a clean PDF or CSV of expenses by property at year end.
