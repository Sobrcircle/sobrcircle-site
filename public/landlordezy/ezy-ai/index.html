How it works

A notice in 30 seconds.

  1. 1

    Ask in plain English.

    “Draft a 24-hour notice. The plumber is coming Thursday afternoon.”

  2. 2

    Review the draft.

    Names, address and dates come from the tenancy record, and the draft cites the section of the Act it relies on. You check it before it goes.

  3. 3

    Send and see it opened.

    One tap sends it by text, and by email once your tenant has agreed to email service. No tenant login, and a clean delivery record.

What’s included

Questions, answered.

Is Ezy giving me legal advice?

No. Ezy drafts documents and answers general questions. For advice on your specific situation, talk to a licensed paralegal.

Will it send anything without me?

No. Ezy drafts for your review. You decide what goes out and when.

What does Ezy AI cost?

$29.99 a month plus $1 per door, or $9.99 a month plus $1 per door with code AI20, locked in for as long as you stay subscribed. Self Management includes three free drafts a month, and Ask Ezy answers three questions a day free.

How is Ask Ezy different from Ezy AI?

Ask Ezy answers Ontario landlord-tenant questions and is free to try. Ezy AI works inside your account, on each tenant profile, and drafts and sends notices and letters.
