Trust is the whole job.

Landlords trust us with their tenants’ information, and tenants trust their landlords. Here’s how LandlordEzy handles that responsibility.

Our principles

Consent first

Applicants authorize every credit check and choose what to share. On-time rent reporting needs the tenant’s consent, recorded and versioned.

Soft inquiries only

Credit checks through LandlordEzy never lower an applicant’s credit score.

Never sold

LandlordEzy doesn’t sell personal information, to anyone, for any purpose.

Delete what we don’t need

ID images used to verify Reference Network members are deleted as soon as verification is done.

Reports that can’t be forged

Every screening report has a unique ID that can be verified on the platform.

Canadian-owned

LandlordEzy is owned and operated in Ontario, under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA).

LTB orders

Research by situation, never by person.

LandlordEzy used to host a searchable collection of Landlord and Tenant Board orders. We took it down: orders were being reposted out of context in ways that followed people well beyond the matter they were about.

The Board now publishes its own order catalogue on Ontario’s open data portal. CaseBuilder helps paralegals and landlords research how the Board decides cases by issue and outcome, not by who was involved, and orders removed at a party’s request stay excluded.

Your choices

Access and correct

You can access and update your information at any time from your account.

Delete your data

Request deletion of your account and personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.

Stop texts

Reply STOP to any LandlordEzy text message to opt out.
