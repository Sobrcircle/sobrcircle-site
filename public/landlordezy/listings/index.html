Questions, answered.

Is listing on EzyRent really free?

Yes. Listings are free and unlimited on every plan, including Free.

What is a flat-fee MLS® listing?

Instead of paying a commission, you pay one flat fee to have your rental listed on REALTOR.ca and the MLS® system for 12 months. You keep control and choose your own tenant.

What does the Legal Guarantee cover?

Non-payment of rent: the team prepares and files the N4 and L1, represents you at the Landlord and Tenant Board and coordinates re-listing. Coverage is 3 months with the $549 listing or 12 months with the $749 listing. Report missed rent within 5 days and use LandlordEzy for rent tracking to stay eligible. Damage, maintenance disputes and government fees are not covered.

Can a Realtor handle everything?

Yes. Full-service tenant placement is one month’s rent plus HST, paid when the tenant is placed, and includes a 12-month placement guarantee and a 12-month Legal Guarantee.
