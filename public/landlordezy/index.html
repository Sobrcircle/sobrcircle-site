Questions, answered.

Is LandlordEzy really free?

Yes. The Free plan covers up to two tenants with rent tracking, reminders, receipts, a tenant portal, pre-screening and free listings. No credit card. Self Management starts at $9.99 a month plus $1 per door when you want automation and Equifax reporting for your whole portfolio.

Is it only for Ontario?

The legal tools, including N4 and N1 notices, the Ontario Standard Lease and LTB workflows, are built for Ontario’s Residential Tenancies Act. Tenant screening, Equifax credit checks and free listings work across Canada.

Does a credit check hurt an applicant’s score?

No. Credit checks through LandlordEzy are soft inquiries, so applicants can apply to several homes without any effect on their score.

Do I still need a property manager or a paralegal?

For most landlords, not for the routine work. LandlordEzy automates collecting and raising rent, reporting it, screening and the paperwork for notices. When a matter is contested, licensed paralegals are a tap away.

Is my tenants’ information safe?

Everything runs over encrypted connections, data is never sold, and ID images used for Reference Network verification are deleted right after review. LandlordEzy is Canadian-owned and operated.

Can someone run it for me?

Yes. VirtualPM Legal Shield has the team collect rent by EFT, issue notices on schedule and give you a monthly paralegal consultation, from $49 a month plus $6 per unit.
