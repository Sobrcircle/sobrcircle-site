Questions, answered.

Do I have to use the Ontario Standard Lease?

For most residential tenancy agreements signed on or after April 30, 2018, yes. Care homes, most social and supportive housing, co-operatives and a few other tenancies are exempt. See the province’s guide.

Can the lease be signed electronically?

Yes. The province’s guide to the Standard Lease says the landlord and tenant can sign electronically if they both agree.

Why does a move-in inspection matter?

If there’s ever a damage dispute, a dated, photo-backed record that the tenant acknowledged is exactly the evidence you’ll want at the Landlord and Tenant Board.
