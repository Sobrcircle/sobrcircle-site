Questions, answered.

What can a three-hour block cover?

Support on one tenant file, such as filing an LTB application, preparing evidence and strategy, with updates and one hearing. The twelve-hour block covers more complex cases or several files.

Is there a guarantee if a paralegal makes a mistake?

Yes. If a paralegal error causes you to lose, you get a full refund and your next eviction handled free.

Do I need a paid plan for legal blocks?

Yes. Paralegal blocks are available to paid LandlordEzy members.

Who provides the legal services?

Landlord Pro Legal SPC, a licensed Ontario paralegal firm. LandlordEzy is the technology platform.
