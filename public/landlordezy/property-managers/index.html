Questions property managers ask

Can I manage properties for multiple owners?

Yes. Create a profile for each owner, assign their properties (including co-owned buildings split by percentage), set per-owner fees, generate monthly statements and invite owners to a read-only portal.

Can we switch from our current software?

Yes. Upload reports, spreadsheets or exports from your current system and AI builds your owners, properties, units and tenants. Concierge onboarding, where the team does the whole import with you, is $159 one time.

Does it replace our CRM and e-signature tools?

For property management work, yes: a lead pipeline, call recaps, AI-written follow-ups, a meeting scheduler and e-signed management agreements are built in.

How does pricing compare with U.S. platforms?

PM Manage is $29.99 a month plus $1 per door in Canadian dollars, with no per-payment or per-signature fees. Buildium, for comparison, starts at US$62 a month and charges per incoming EFT and per e-signature on its entry plan (as of October 2026).

Does it handle Ontario LTB notices?

Yes. N4 and N1 notices are generated in one click from the ledger, on the forms and rules in force on the day you serve.
