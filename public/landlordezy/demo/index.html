Demo questions

Do I have to talk to a salesperson?

No. The demo is self-serve and instant. There’s no call and no booking.

Is it free?

Completely. No credit card and nothing to install. Paid actions inside the demo, like credit checks, are simulated.

How long do I have?

Your private demo stays open for 48 hours, then deletes itself. One demo per person.

Is any real data involved?

No. Every tenant in the demo is fictional, and no real messages or documents are sent to anyone.
