SobrCircle: a free, private recovery app

Recovery is harder alone. You don't have to be. SobrCircle is a private space for the people who show up, built in Windsor, Ontario by someone in recovery.

Find recovery meetings near you

195,000+ meetings on one map, including AA, NA and CA meetings, with the time, the place and live navigation to the door. Drive, walk, bike or take transit. New city, same recovery. Find meetings near you.

Your circle, people you choose

Your circle is people you invite with a private code. Start with the people who answer at 2 AM.

Daily check ins

Some days you need to say it out loud. A check in is a moment of honesty for you and for someone who needs to hear they're not the only one.

A private journal

A safe place to process the hard days and track your progress. Never visible to anyone.

Free, with no ads and no data selling

Every core feature is free. No ads, no data selling, and you can sign up without a credit card.

Why SobrCircle exists

Founder Ben Moradi struggled with addiction for most of his life. When he started getting better, there was no space built for the daily part of recovery, so he built one. His poetry memoir, Twenty Four, tells the story behind the app.
